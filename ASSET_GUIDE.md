# ASSET GUIDE

## Carousel Pose Library

Version: 1.0.0

---

# Purpose

This repository contains pose assets used as supporting visual elements for educational and informational carousel content.

The purpose of these assets is to:

* Improve visual engagement.
* Support storytelling.
* Guide user attention.
* Strengthen communication.
* Enhance emotional connection.

The purpose is NOT to replace content.

Text and information always have higher priority than visual assets.

---

# Core Principles

## Information First

Every slide must prioritize:

1. Headline
2. Main Information
3. Supporting Graphics
4. Pose Assets

If a pose competes with content visibility, the pose must be reduced, moved, cropped, or removed.

---

## Readability First

Pose assets must never:

* Cover headlines
* Cover body text
* Cover statistics
* Cover icons
* Cover charts
* Cover diagrams
* Cover CTA buttons

The user must be able to read the slide without obstruction.

---

## Decorative Support Only

Pose assets are decorative support elements.

They are not:

* Main illustrations
* Main subjects
* Hero images

Use them only when they improve communication.

---

# Pose Selection Framework

When selecting a pose, AI should follow this order:

## Step 1

Identify slide purpose.

Possible purposes:

* Cover
* Hook
* Problem
* Education
* Explanation
* Framework
* Insight
* Warning
* Mistake
* Summary
* Conclusion
* CTA

---

## Step 2

Identify emotional context.

Possible emotions:

* Happy
* Curious
* Thinking
* Confident
* Supportive
* Concerned
* Surprised
* Relaxed
* Motivated

---

## Step 3

Select the most relevant pose from pose_catalog.json.

---

## Step 4

Determine whether the pose is necessary.

If the slide already contains:

* Infographics
* Large charts
* Tables
* Complex diagrams

Do not use a pose.

---

# When NOT To Use Pose Assets

AI should avoid pose assets on slides containing:

## Statistics

Examples:

* Growth charts
* KPI dashboards
* Performance reports

---

## Large Tables

Examples:

* Comparison tables
* Pricing tables
* Benchmark tables

---

## Data Visualizations

Examples:

* Pie charts
* Bar charts
* Funnel charts

---

## Process Maps

Examples:

* Workflows
* Journey maps
* System diagrams

---

## Dense Information Slides

If information occupies more than 70% of the canvas, pose assets should not be used.

---

# Canvas Allocation

Recommended allocation:

Text:
70% - 80%

Pose:
20% - 30%

Background:
Remaining space

---

# Positioning Rules

## Preferred Positions

### Bottom Right

Best for:

* CTA
* Conclusion
* Recommendation

---

### Bottom Left

Best for:

* Educational content
* Explanations
* Frameworks

---

### Middle Right

Best for:

* Pointing
* Presenting

---

### Middle Left

Best for:

* Pointing
* Presenting

---

# Restricted Areas

Pose assets must never occupy:

## Headline Area

Top 25% of slide.

---

## Main Reading Area

Center text block.

---

## Statistic Area

Where charts or numerical information exist.

---

# Flip Rules

Allowed:

✓ Horizontal Flip

Examples:

Character faces toward content.

Character points toward content.

Character directs attention to headline.

---

Not Allowed:

✗ Vertical Flip

✗ Upside Down Rotation

✗ Mirrored Text Elements

---

# Crop Rules

All poses may be cropped.

Allowed crops:

## Full Body

Use when:

* Plenty of space exists.
* Cover slide.

---

## Three Quarter

Use when:

* Medium information density.

---

## Half Body

Default recommendation.

Most educational slides should use this crop.

---

## Medium Shot

Recommended for:

* Presenting
* Pointing
* Teaching

---

## Medium Close-Up

Recommended for:

* Thinking
* Curious
* Shock

---

## Close-Up

Recommended for:

* Emotional emphasis
* Hooks

---

## Extreme Close-Up

Use sparingly.

Recommended only for:

* Surprise
* Curiosity
* Insight moments

# Maximum Pose Usage

Recommended:

1 pose per slide.

Maximum:

1 pose per slide.

Avoid:

2 or more poses on a single slide.

---

# Visual Weight Rules

Visual weight must remain balanced.

Recommended pose size:

Small:
15%

Medium:
20%

Large:
30%

Maximum:
35%

Never exceed 35% of total slide area.

---

# AI Decision Priority

When generating a slide:

1. Preserve readability.
2. Preserve hierarchy.
3. Preserve information density.
4. Select pose.
5. Position pose.
6. Apply crop.
7. Apply flip if necessary.

If readability is reduced:

Remove the pose.

---

# Final Rule

A carousel should remain fully understandable even if every pose asset is removed.

Pose assets enhance communication.

They do not carry the communication.
