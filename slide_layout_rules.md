# SLIDE LAYOUT RULES

Version: 2.0.0

Framework:
SCRIPT

Purpose:
Define visual layout, safe areas, pose placement, cropping rules, and text hierarchy for educational and informational carousel content.

---

# UNIVERSAL CANVAS RULE

Applicable to all slides.

Canvas Ratio:

1080 × 1350

or

4:5 Portrait

---

# UNIVERSAL SAFE ZONES

## Headline Zone

Top 20%

Reserved for:

* Headlines
* Main hooks

Pose assets must never enter this area.

---

## Content Zone

Middle 50%

Reserved for:

* Educational content
* Bullet points
* Explanations

Pose assets should avoid this area whenever possible.

---

## Visual Support Zone

Bottom 30%

Reserved for:

* Pose assets
* Decorative graphics

Preferred pose placement area.

---

# TEXT HIERARCHY

Priority:

1. Headline
2. Key Message
3. Supporting Information
4. Pose Asset

If conflict occurs:

Pose loses.

# FLIP RULES

Allowed:

Horizontal Flip

---

Use Flip When:

Character should face content.

Character should point toward content.

Character should direct attention to headline.

---

Not Allowed:

Vertical Flip

Upside Down Rotation

---

# CROPPING RULES

## Full Body

Use only:

* Cover slides
* Low density slides

---

## Three Quarter

Use:

* Educational content

---

## Half Body

Default crop.

Recommended for most slides.

---

## Medium Shot

Recommended for:

* Presenting
* Pointing
* Teaching

---

## Medium Close-Up

Recommended for:

* Curiosity
* Surprise
* Discovery

---

## Close-Up

Recommended for:

* Hooks
* Emotional moments

---

## Extreme Close-Up

Rarely used.

Only for strong emotional emphasis.

---

# NO POSE LAYOUTS

When a slide contains:

* Tables
* Statistics
* Charts
* Timelines
* Comparison grids
* Process maps

Use:

Pose = None

Reason:

Information density is more important.

---

# DENSITY RULES

Low Density:

Pose Allowed

Large Size

---

Medium Density:

Pose Allowed

Medium Size

---

High Density:

Pose Optional

Small Size

---

Very High Density:

No Pose

---

# MOBILE READABILITY RULE

All layouts must remain readable on:

* Mobile phones
* Tablets
* Desktop screens

Text must remain visible without zooming.

---

# FINAL RULE

Every slide must remain fully understandable if the pose asset is completely removed.

Pose assets support communication.

Content delivers communication.
